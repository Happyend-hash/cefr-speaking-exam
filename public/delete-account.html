Hisobni o‘chirish

English Hub Family · englishhub.uz va Android ilovasi

English Hub hisobingizni va undagi ma’lumotlarni istalgan vaqtda o‘zingiz butunlay o‘chira olasiz.

Qanday o‘chiriladi

  1. Ilovada yoki englishhub.uz saytida hisobingizga kiring.
  2. Profil bo‘limini oching va pastdagi «Hisobni o‘chirish» tugmasini bosing.
  3. Tasdiqlash uchun hisobingiz emailini yozing va «Hisobni butunlay o‘chirish»ni bosing.

Hisobingizga kira olmasangiz, ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan emailingizdan xappyend@gmail.com manziliga «Hisobni o‘chirish» deb yozing — 7 kun ichida o‘chiramiz.

Nima o‘chiriladi

  • Hisob: ism, email, parol, profil rasmi va taxallus;
  • barcha speaking va writing urinishlari, ovoz yozuvlari, matnlar va natijalar;
  • qolgan mocklar va Premium;
  • do‘stlar, chatlar, postlar, izohlar, xabarlar va yuborilgan rasmlar;
  • reyting va o‘yin ochkolari, yordam markazidagi yozishmalar.

Ma’lumotlar darhol o‘chiriladi. Buni ortga qaytarib bo‘lmaydi.

Nima saqlanib qoladi

  • To‘lov yozuvlari (summa, sana, paket, ism va email) — buxgalteriya va soliq hisobi uchun.
  • Emailingizning qaytarib bo‘lmaydigan shifrlangan izi — shu email bilan qayta ro‘yxatdan o‘tganda bepul mock qayta berilmasligi uchun. Emailning o‘zi saqlanmaydi.

Delete your account

English Hub Family · englishhub.uz and the Android app

You can delete your English Hub account and its data yourself at any time.

How to delete it

  1. Sign in in the app or on englishhub.uz.
  2. Open Profil and tap “Hisobni o‘chirish” (Delete account) at the bottom.
  3. Type your account’s email to confirm and tap “Hisobni butunlay o‘chirish”.

If you cannot sign in, email xappyend@gmail.com from the address you registered with, asking to delete your account. We delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • The account: name, email, password, profile picture and nickname;
  • all speaking and writing attempts, recordings, transcripts and results;
  • remaining mocks and Premium;
  • friends, chats, posts, comments, messages and photos you sent;
  • leaderboard and game points, and your support conversation.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is kept

  • Payment records (amount, date, package, name and email), for accounting and tax purposes.
  • A one-way hash of your email, so signing up again with it does not give another free mock. The email itself is not kept.
