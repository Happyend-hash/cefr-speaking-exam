English Hub — CEFR (Multilevel) gapirish va yozish bo‘yicha mock imtihonlar sayti. Bu sahifada qanday ma’lumot to‘planishi, nima uchun ishlatilishi va kim bilan ulashilishi tushuntiriladi.

Qanday ma’lumot to‘planadi

  • Hisob: ism, familiya, elektron pochta, parol (shifrlangan holda saqlanadi). Google orqali kirsangiz — Google bergan ism va elektron pochta.
  • Imtihon javoblari: gapirish yozuvlari (ovoz), ularning matni, yozma ishlaringiz, ballar va izohlar.
  • Sayt ichidagi faollik: chat xabarlari, gapirish klubi yozuvlari, o‘yin natijalari, reyting uchun taxallus, Premium rasmi.
  • Ota-onalar sahifasi: o‘quv markazi o‘quvchilarining ismi, rasmi, darajasi, uy vazifasi natijalari, davomat va to‘lov holati — ularni ustoz kiritadi va faqat shu oila 6 xonali kod bilan ko‘radi.
  • Texnik ma’lumot: IP manzil va brauzer turi — xavfsizlik va suiiste’molni oldini olish uchun.

Nima uchun ishlatiladi

  • Hisobingizga kirish, imtihonlarni baholash va natijalarni ko‘rsatish.
  • Ustozga va ota-onalarga o‘quvchining natijalarini ko‘rsatish.
  • Saytni xavfsiz va ishlaydigan holda saqlash.

Ma’lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.

Kim bilan ulashiladi

Saytni ishlatish uchun quyidagi xizmatlardan foydalanamiz; ular ma’lumotni faqat shu ish uchun qayta ishlaydi:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — javoblarni baholash;
  • OpenAI — ovozni matnga aylantirish;
  • Microsoft Azure — talaffuzni baholash;
  • Google — Google orqali kirish va «AI Ustoz» ovozli suhbati (Gemini);
  • MongoDB Atlas — ma’lumotlar bazasi; Railway — sayt serveri; Brevo — elektron xatlar.

Qancha vaqt saqlanadi

  • Hisob va natijalar — hisobingiz o‘chirilguncha. Mock urinishlarini o‘zingiz o‘chirishingiz mumkin; ovoz yozuvlari ham birga o‘chadi.
  • Gapirish klubi yozuvlari — 7 kun; chat xabarlari — 30 kun.

Sizning huquqlaringiz

Ma’lumotlaringizni ko‘rish, tuzatish yoki hisobingizni butunlay o‘chirishni so‘rashingiz mumkin. Bolalar haqidagi ma’lumotni ota-ona ustozdan o‘chirishni so‘rashi mumkin.

Privacy policy

English Hub · Last updated: 23 October 2026

English Hub is a website for CEFR (Multilevel) speaking and writing mock exams. This page explains what information we collect, why, and who it is shared with.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address and password (stored encrypted). If you sign in with Google, the name and email address Google shares with us.
  • Exam answers: speaking recordings, their transcripts, your writing, scores and feedback.
  • Activity on the site: chat messages, speaking-club recordings, game results, leaderboard nickname, Premium picture.
  • Parents’ page: course students’ names, photos, levels, homework results, attendance and payment status — entered by the teacher and visible only to that family with their 6-digit code.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser type, for security and abuse prevention.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in, mark your exams and show your results.
  • To show a student’s progress to their teacher and parents.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who it is shared with

We use these services to run the site; they process data only for that purpose:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — marking answers;
  • OpenAI — turning speech into text;
  • Microsoft Azure — pronunciation assessment;
  • Google — Sign in with Google and the “AI Ustoz” voice tutor (Gemini);
  • MongoDB Atlas — database; Railway — hosting; Brevo — email.

How long we keep it

  • Account and results — until your account is deleted. You can delete your own mock attempts; their recordings are deleted with them.
  • Speaking-club recordings — 7 days; chat messages — 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your information, or to delete your account entirely. Parents can ask the teacher to remove their child’s information.
